About Stealth Security

An Arkansas-based security and technology company providing protection for businesses and homes throughout the state.

We focus on reliable installation, responsive local support and long-term customer relationships.

Most companies hire one vendor for fire alarms, another for cameras and a third for computers. Stealth Security handles security, life safety and IT under one roof, so there is one local team that knows your whole building and one number to call.

We are based in Clarksville, Arkansas, and serve customers across the River Valley and beyond.
