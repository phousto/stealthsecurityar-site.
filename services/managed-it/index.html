What we install

Products we work with

ByteSpeed

computers and servers

Desktops, laptops, workstations and servers built to order in the U.S. by ByteSpeed, which supplies schools, healthcare, government and businesses. ByteSpeed computers come with a 5-year warranty and free lifetime U.S.-based tech support, and we spec, configure, image and install them for you.

Gravity by ByteSpeed

esports and high-performance PCs

Custom gaming and high-performance PCs for school esports teams and computer labs, plus esports furniture, Logitech G gear, streaming and shoutcasting equipment, and team-logo builds. The same machines handle Adobe, CAD, engineering and AI work.

Ubiquiti UniFi networking

gateways, switches and Wi-Fi

UniFi gateways, switches and Wi-Fi access points managed together from one dashboard and app. It's business-grade networking at a small-business price, and it ties in with UniFi cameras and door access so your whole building runs on one system.

Wi-Fi and backup internet

Ruckus and Cradlepoint

Ruckus Wi-Fi that covers the whole building, from classrooms and offices to warehouses, and Cradlepoint LTE and 5G routers that keep your registers, phones and alarm connected when your main internet goes down. Cradlepoint also puts internet on job sites and in vehicles.

Lenovo

business laptops and desktops

Lenovo ThinkPad laptops and ThinkCentre desktops for offices that want a widely available business-class brand. We set them up, enroll them in management and keep them patched.

Malwarebytes

endpoint protection

Malwarebytes business endpoint protection (now sold as ThreatDown) guards your computers against malware and ransomware. We deploy it, watch its alerts and handle cleanup if something gets through.

Offsite backups

encrypted, tested, recoverable

Automatic backups of servers and key computers copied off-site and encrypted, so a fire, theft, failed drive or ransomware attack doesn't take your data with it. We monitor every backup job and test restores, because a backup only counts if it comes back.
