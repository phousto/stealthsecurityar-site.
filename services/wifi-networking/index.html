Wi-Fi & Networking

Business Wi-Fi and Networking

Fast, reliable Wi-Fi in every room, a secure network behind it, and one local team to call when something drops.

Request a Free QuoteCall 1-479-746-8298

Most Wi-Fi problems come from a single consumer router trying to cover a whole building. We replace it with business access points placed where your people actually work, a proper gateway and firewall, and switches that power your access points and cameras.

We build on Ubiquiti UniFi, so your Wi-Fi, network, cameras and door access can all be managed from one dashboard. Staff, guest, camera and payment devices each get their own network, so a guest on your Wi-Fi can't reach your computers.

Wi-Fi

  • Indoor and outdoor access points
  • Guest Wi-Fi with its own password
  • Coverage for shops, barns and warehouses
  • Site survey and access point placement

Network

  • PoE switches for access points and cameras
  • Separate staff, guest and camera networks

Between buildings

What we install

Products we work with

Ubiquiti UniFi

gateways, switches and Wi-Fi

UniFi Cloud Gateways, PoE switches and Wi-Fi 6 and 7 access points, all managed from one app. Business-grade performance without enterprise license fees, and it ties in with UniFi Protect cameras and UniFi Access doors.

UniFi building bridges

wireless links between buildings

Connect a shop, barn, second office or gate to your main network without trenching cable. A pair of UniFi bridge radios links buildings hundreds of feet to miles apart, so cameras and Wi-Fi in the outbuilding work like they're in the main building.

Ruckus and Cradlepoint

high-density Wi-Fi and backup internet

Ruckus access points for busy classrooms and large venues, and Cradlepoint LTE and 5G routers that keep registers, phones and alarms online when your main internet goes down.
